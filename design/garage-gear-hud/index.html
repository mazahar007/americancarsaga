[ 04 ]  FAQ

QUESTIONS.

Where do I pay?

Checkout happens securely on our store partner’s site, not on American Car Saga, so we never see your card details.

How long does delivery take?

Items are made to order, so production plus shipping usually takes about one to two weeks. The exact estimate is shown at checkout.

Can I return an item?

If an item arrives damaged or misprinted we will replace it. Because everything is made just for you, other returns are limited. Full details are on the store’s policy page.

Does the preview match the real product?

The preview is an illustration. Real fabric, ink and colours can look slightly different on screen and in person.
