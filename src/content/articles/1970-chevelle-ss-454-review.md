In 1970, General Motors lifted its limit on engine size in mid-size cars, and Chevrolet responded with the biggest engine it could fit: the 454. In LS6 form it carried a factory rating of 450 horsepower — one of the highest of the classic muscle era.

## What it is like to drive

Even the more common LS5 version delivers huge, effortless torque. Press the pedal and the car simply surges forward. Around town, it is surprisingly civilized, with a soft ride and comfortable bench or bucket seats.

## Buyer's checklist

- Confirm the engine and build documentation — clones are common.
- Inspect the frame, rear window channel and trunk for rust.
- Budget for good tires and brakes; this is a heavy, powerful car.

## Verdict

The 1970 Chevelle SS 454 is the muscle car at full volume. If you can afford one and love the big-block experience, few classics deliver more drama.
