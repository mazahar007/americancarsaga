A good car cover is one of the least expensive ways to protect a classic car's paint. A bad one can do more harm than good. Here is how to choose.

## Indoor or outdoor?

- **Indoor covers** are soft and stretchy. They keep off dust and protect against bumps, but they are not waterproof.
- **Outdoor covers** use several layers to resist rain, sun and bird droppings. The best are *breathable* so moisture does not get trapped against the paint.

## What to look for

1. **A soft inner lining** — fleece or cotton touches your paint, not plastic.
2. **Breathability** — this matters more than "100% waterproof."
3. **A good fit** — a loose cover flaps in the wind and rubs the finish.
4. **Elastic hems and straps** — so it stays put.

## A quick tip

Always wash and dry your car before covering it. Dust trapped under a cover acts like fine sandpaper.
